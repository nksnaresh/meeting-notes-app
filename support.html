Meeting Notes Support

For Apple Watch, iPhone and Mac

Need help or found a problem? Open a support request and we'll reply there. Please don't include meeting content or API keys.

Contact support

Recording

Start with one tap on Apple Watch. Add the Record complication to a watch face (touch and hold the face › Edit › Complications), or add the Record Meeting control to Control Center or the Action Button. On iPhone, use the Record tab; on Mac, press ⇧⌘R or use the menu bar.

Recording stops by itself after 5 minutes without speech (change this in Settings), and the silence at the end is trimmed.

Watch recordings arrive on iPhone automatically. Keep the iPhone app installed. Recordings are checked byte for byte before the Watch deletes its copy, so nothing is lost if the transfer is interrupted.

Transcripts and notes

Transcripts, speaker labels and notes are made on your device. Notes are written by Apple Intelligence where it's available, otherwise by the built-in extractor. To use OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or AssemblyAI instead, add your own key in Settings › Your AI keys.

If transcription says the language isn't available, turn on that language for dictation (iPhone: Settings › General › Keyboard; Mac: System Settings › Keyboard) or pick another meeting language in the app's Settings.

Your meetings in iCloud

Every meeting is a folder in iCloud Drive › Watch Meeting Notes, with the audio, transcript and notes as ordinary files. Meetings appear on all your devices signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on.

Mac

Drop any audio or video file — Zoom or Teams recordings, voice memos, podcasts — on the window to transcribe it. Every action item from every meeting is in Action Items; search covers every transcript.

Privacy

We don't run a server that stores your meetings and we can't see them. Read the privacy policy.

Recording laws differ by place. Please tell people when you are recording.
